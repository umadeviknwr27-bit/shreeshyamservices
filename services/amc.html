Clear expectations

What your AMC includes — and what it doesn't

We explain the scope before activation so there are no surprises later.

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled checks and the cleaning tasks listed in the selected plan, subject to safe access and appliance condition.

Priority booking

AMC customers receive priority when appointment slots are available. This is not a guaranteed same-day service.

Repair estimates

If a fault or replacement part is found, we explain the issue and share a separate estimate before paid repair work.

Important plan conditions

  • Each contract runs for 12 months from its confirmed start date.
  • Plans are subject to service-area and appliance eligibility checks.
  • Existing faults must be disclosed and may need repair before AMC activation.
  • Spare parts, consumables and refrigerant/gas are charged separately.
  • Major repairs and replacement components are not included.
  • Damage from misuse, accidents, pests, fire, flooding or external electrical events is excluded.
  • Installation, shifting and relocation are not preventive-maintenance visits.
  • Commercial appliances require a separate quotation.
  • Payment, cancellation and rescheduling terms are shared in writing before activation.

The written contract issued to the customer will specify the exact number of visits, covered appliance(s), eligible labour discount (if any), exclusions and payment terms.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does AMC cover every repair free of charge?

No. The plans focus on preventive maintenance. Parts, refrigerant, consumables and major repairs are charged separately unless a specific written contract expressly says otherwise.

Can I enrol an appliance that is already faulty?

Please tell us about the issue first. We may need to inspect and repair the appliance before an AMC can begin.

When does my AMC start?

Your 12-month term starts on the activation date confirmed in your contract, after serviceability checks and payment.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Dwarka and selected West Delhi locations, including Uttam Nagar and Najafgarh. Share your full location so we can confirm availability.

How do I book a maintenance visit?

Contact us by phone or WhatsApp and share your AMC ID. We will confirm an available appointment slot.

Not sure which plan is right for your home?

Tell us which appliances you have. We’ll check eligibility and recommend the right coverage.
