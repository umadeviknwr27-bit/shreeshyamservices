Common microwave problems we fix

Sparking Inside

Turntable Not Rotating

Worn turntable motor or a misaligned coupler.

Door Not Closing / Latching

Broken door latch or safety interlock switch.

Display / Panel Not Working

Faulty control board or membrane keypad.

Loud Noise While Running

Worn turntable motor or cooling fan needing replacement.
